❓ How Power Hour works

The basics

Tap the ＋ button at bottom-right, give it a name, a time estimate and a priority from P1 (urgent) to P5 (someday). Tasks sit in one list grouped by priority. If the name looks like something already on your list, you'll be asked before it's added. Under More options you can add a tag, a deadline, a start time, a date, notes, order rules and recurrence. Finished tasks drop into Done today at the bottom, where you can undo them.

Gestures

  • Tap a task to edit it (or delete it from there).
  • Swipe right or left for a quick action. Choose what each does in Settings → Customise → Gestures.
  • Interrupted mid-task? Tap ⏳ Bank time on the timer. The minutes you spent are kept and the rest is planned later.
  • Switch features on or off, and edit the time shortcuts and morning-question answers, in Settings → Customise → Features & shortcuts.
  • Add a morning question to a task (under More options) and it asks you the first time you open the app each day: normal, do first, urgent, or not today. Those answers last for that day only.
  • On the plan screen, drag a task (or tap ▲ / ▼) between Planned and Left out, then say why. After two similar moves I adjust future plans within a priority; see Settings → Customise → What I've learned.
  • Press and hold, then drag up or down to change priority; hold near the left edge to reorder within a priority.

Start a Power Hour

Tap ▶ Start a Power Hour and say how many minutes you have. The app picks the best mix of tasks: P1 first, then whatever time is left flows down. Must-dos, escalated tasks and deadlines get priority within a level, and equal tasks are shuffled so the same ones don't always win. Tasks you've marked Do it in bits soak up spare time in small chunks, and a do-first routine (a do-first task plus everything chained after it) is always planned before anything else and carries on in the next session if it doesn't all fit. The plan shows what was picked, what was left out and why. You can edit or untick anything before you start. Need longer? Tap ＋ Add more time on the timer. During the timer, ✕ ends the session (after a check) and the bar under the clock shows what's done and what's left.

Order rules

In a task's options, Do after and Do before link it to another task, or pin it as 🥇 first or 🏁 last. The picker pulls in what a task depends on, and the timer always respects the order.

Tasks that sit too long

A pending task moves up a priority after a set number of days, and a P1 that stays put is flagged ❗ must do, which is hard to untick. Timings are in Settings → Customise → Escalation timing, where you can also see aging tasks. Editing only restarts a task's clock if you change its priority, date or age.

Tags and Quick tidy

Tags (🎧 No hands, 🏃 Errands, 🚶 Move around) let you filter what the timer picks. If you've been away for 5+ hours, a Quick tidy prompt offers to tag anything untagged.

Steps and daily goal nudges

Set a step goal and log steps; if you fall behind, a Walk task appears automatically. In Customise → Daily goal nudges you can set goals like water or stretching. With Move around selected, and once your P1s are done, a surprise 1-minute break appears between tasks. It's deliberately hard to skip.

Dates, times and recurring

Give a task a date and it stays out of sight until then. Must be done by sets AM, PM or a time; Don't pick up before keeps it out of the timer until later. A task with a must be done by time is bumped up as it gets close, and by more if the time passes. Recurring tasks come back fresh when you clear the day, either every N days or on set weekdays. Do it in bits (under More options) breaks a long task like “Tidy room, 40 min” into 5–30 minute chunks with a progress bar; progress carries over to the next day.

Ending the day

Tap 🌇 End of day for highlights or the full list, then clear the day. If you forget, the app asks "How did yesterday go?" the next morning. Past days, habit consistency and your last 7 days are all in Settings → History.

Backup and updates

Settings → Data & backup has Cloud backup (Google sign-in, best used in Safari), a copyable Backup code, bulk add, export and automatic daily snapshots. A banner appears when a new version is ready. Appearance switches between dark, light and matching your phone, and Timer alerts turns the end-of-task chime on or off.
